Jaipur


Jaipur, known as the Pink City, is the capital of Rajasthan, famous for its historic palaces, grand hilltop forts, vibrant culture, and rich royal heritage.

Top Attractions

  1. Hawa Mahal – The iconic Palace of Breeze featuring intricate lattice windows.
  2. Amber Fort – A majestic hilltop fortress blending Rajput and Mughal architecture.
  3. City Palace – A royal residence complex featuring museums, courtyards, and gardens.
  4. Jantar Mantar – A UNESCO World Heritage site housing historic astronomical instruments.
  5. Nahargarh Fort – A scenic fort offering breathtaking panoramic views of the city.

Must-Try Foods

  • Dal Baati Churma – A signature dish of baked wheat balls served with spiced lentils and ghee.
  • Pyaaz Kachori – A crisp, deep-fried pastry filled with a savory spiced onion mixture.
  • Ghevar – A classic honeycomb-textured sweet soaked in sugar syrup.
  • Laal Maas – A traditional fiery mutton curry prepared with Rajasthan's local red chilies.
  • Lassi – A rich, creamy yogurt drink served in traditional clay cups.
